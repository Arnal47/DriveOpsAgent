# Brake Validation Notes
## Failsafe criteria
A CAN timeout from the brake controller requires failsafe activation within 200 ms. Wheel-speed mismatch is diagnostic evidence but does not alone mandate failsafe.
## Pressure response
When requested brake pressure exceeds measured pressure by more than 20 bar for 300 ms, record C1234 under-response.
